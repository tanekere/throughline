# Try this

What changed in 0.0.4, and where to try it in this test space. Changes made
here are thrown away the next time the space is reset.

## Edit a task from the task list
Open [[Projects/Website]] and scroll to **Tasks**.

- Each link shows the task's due date after it, red once past.
- Click a task's icon, or its date: a panel opens with the state, Due and
  Scheduled (with a calendar) and Progress. Every change is saved at once.
- Change a task from Planned to Doing there and watch its card move on the
  board above at the same moment.
- **Blocked by**: open "write the launch post" and see what blocks it; × clears
  it, Choose… picks another task. The card on the board shows "⛔ …".
- Escape, Done or a click outside closes the panel.

## The board
On [[Projects/Website]]:

- Open a card: the same date and progress fields are at the top of it.
- Cards with subtasks show a progress ring. Progress comes from the subtasks
  until you set it; × goes back to the subtasks.
- There is no state dropdown on cards any more; drag them between columns.

On [[Projects/Office move]]:

- **Hide backlog**, top right of To do, hides the backlog card. It is on every
  board and the choice holds for all of them: [[Projects/Website]] shows the
  button too, already switched. Reload and it stays.
- **New task**, at the bottom of To do, asks for a name and adds the task to the
  board and to the page's Tasks list.
- No thin grey marks above or below the board any more.

## Speed
- Open any project: the task list icons appear right away instead of up to a
  second later.
- On [[index]], filter the Focus list by project: the total next to "Focus"
  follows the filter.
